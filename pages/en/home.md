Welcome to my website.

My name is **Francisco Javier Falcó Benavent**. I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Mathematical Analysis at the University of Valencia, where I have worked since 2017. In addition to teaching mathematical analysis in several degrees, I teach science outreach courses through the University of Valencia Extension Service.

My research focuses on **functional analysis and the geometry of Banach spaces**, where I have contributed to several areas.
