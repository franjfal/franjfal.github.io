This is the editing room of **The Series Finale**. In the closing class it is opened on the classroom computer, projected: the **Pooling** tab has one block per group, where each group dictates its finding and the values of its shot, and the room builds the class trailer from them. The other tabs (case file, checked exercises, series lab, free editing) are optional for work at home: all the evidence is on paper.
