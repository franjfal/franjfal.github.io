Density of polynomials whose Aron–Berner extensions attain their norms

A Lean 4 formalization, with Mathlib, of the article Density of polynomials whose Aron–Berner extensions attain their norms by J. Falcó. Every result below is proved without sorry and depends only on the standard axioms propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound. The scalar field is any RCLike field, so each statement holds over \mathbb R and over \mathbb C.

The theorems

  • Theorem thm:main (polynomial Lindenstrauss theorem): LindenstraussPolynomials.­polynomial_lindenstrauss, and its density form LindenstraussPolynomials.­polynomial_lindenstrauss_dense.

  • Theorem thm:vector (vector-valued version): LindenstraussPolynomials.­polynomial_lindenstrauss_vector.

  • Lemma lem:isometry (Davie–Gamelin): LindenstraussPolynomials.­polyNorm_abExt and LindenstraussPolynomials.­polyNorm_abExtV.

  • Lemma lem:K: LindenstraussPolynomials.­lemmaK.

  • Lemma lem:geometry and Remark rem:real: LindenstraussPolynomials.­geometry_lemma and LindenstraussPolynomials.­geometry_lemma_real.

How it is organized

The modules follow the article: polynomials and their norms, polarization, the canonical extension and its norm preservation, the estimate for nonnegative polynomials, the substitutions and the geometric lemma, and then the perturbation scheme — step sizes, construction, limit, deficits, ordered compositions — and the passage to the bidual along an ultrafilter. As the final note of the article suggests, the formal proof follows the vector-valued scheme of Section 3 and obtains the scalar case by taking Y=\mathbb K.

Conventions

  • A polynomial is a function P\colon X\to Y with P(x)=L(x,\ldots,x) for a continuous n-linear L.

  • \|P\| is the supremum of \|P(x)\| over the closed unit ball.

  • The canonical extension extends the last variable first, as in the article.
